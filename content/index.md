# BAN 201 — Introduction to Business Analytics

- Nichols College
- Fall 2026, section 04
- Tuesday and Friday, 9:25–10:40

These pages are the lecture notes for the first four weeks of BAN 201. The course introduces data, analytics, and intelligent automation, and it shows how each one can change a business decision. You will start by learning how to describe what has already happened, then tell that story to someone who does not work with spreadsheets every day.

The syllabus on Canvas is the official schedule. These notes follow that schedule for Weeks 1–4.

## Learning objectives for these four weeks

By the end of Week 4, you should be able to:

- Explain what business analytics is, and name the four kinds of analytics questions.
- Tell a strategic decision from a tactical decision and an operational decision.
- Describe a data set in plain language: what was measured, and whether each column is a category or a number.
- Use Excel to sort, filter, highlight, and summarize data.
- Write a short finding that a manager can use, and say what the finding does **not** yet prove.
- Give a three-minute briefing on what the data show.

Later in the semester the course moves to charts in Excel, Power BI dashboards, regression, AI, Power Automate, and a short introduction to Python. Everything in those weeks depends on the habit you build now: start with a question, describe the data carefully, and say only what the data can support.

## How to use these notes

Read the page for the current week before class, or follow it on your laptop during class. Each week has two class meetings. Boxes marked **In class** are pauses for you to talk or try something. Boxes marked **Check** are short questions. Write the answers in your notes.

Bring a laptop with Microsoft Excel starting in Week 2.

## A note on generative AI

The syllabus allows generative AI tools when an assignment says you may use them. If you use one, cite the tool, keep a copy of your prompts and the responses, and be ready to explain every number and sentence you submit. Some class work is there so you can learn the skill yourself. On those parts, do the Excel steps with your own hands.
